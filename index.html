Bhashamites, Now World Over.

Bhashyam has completed over two and a half decades in the realm of education, and along the way the institution has produced numerous achievers and inspirers who continue to make their mark across the globe today.

What started as an initiative to impart education to enable success, has today taken shape and has spread into a pedagogic institution that is providing children a head-start to become our alumni who shall inspire value and vision in the future. As we are proud of our past generation of students, we look forward to this generation of achievers as well, who we believe will be the next set of Bhashyamites dotting the world achievers' map.

Bhashyam EDU Initiatives

STRENGTHENING THE ROOTS OF OUR BRANCHES.

To keep pace with increasing academic aspirations and changes in the world, there are four Bhashyam schools, with each focusing on a certain age-group of students.

To set a strong learning foundation from the formative years itself, the group has set up Bhashyam Little Champs with classes from KG to Grade V. The exclusivelydesigned programs offered here set a perfect launchpad for children to aim for higher levels of knowledge and learning after primary education.

Read More

Bhashyam IIT Foundation Academy delivers a focused and result-oriented 5-year integrated foundation program from Grade VI onwards to nurture students towards success in JEE (Main, Advanced), BITSAT, IIST, KVPY and NEST.

Read More

To suit the new Medical competitive education format in the country, Bhashyam initiated MEDIAIMS, a dedicated Medical Excellence Programme that is aimed to groom students for NEET from their VIII Grade.

Bhashyam Rama Krishna

Chairman

Mr. Rama Krishna comes to school daily. He comes to learn. His subjects are students' needs, their hurdles and their fears and doubts. His teachers are the experiences and the outcome of every single issue. In his words, "Education begins the day you think you've not learnt anything". We can only say - happy learning.

"I always believe and keep telling my students that a positive attitude will take you to great altitudes. The intention to contribute to the development of the society is primary. The action will naturally be reciprocal. Bhashyam was born with a strong commitment for a social renaissance, the parents and well wishers took the movement forward."
